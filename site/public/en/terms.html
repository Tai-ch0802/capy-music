1. What capy is

capy is open-source software maintained by Tai (GitHub: Tai-ch0802) as an individual. It searches music, controls playback, reads playlists from Spotify, Apple Music and a local music library, and syncs or transfers them to Spotify, Apple Music and the local library (on Apple Music, only playlists you created yourself). It runs on your own computer; we provide no online service, accounts or hosting. By downloading, installing or using capy you agree to these terms.
